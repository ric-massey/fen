Fen

An artificial organism with a body, drives it must regulate, and damage it carries. The colour strip is its own vocabulary — symbols it derived from experience, not from anything human.
symbol—
Red half is warm, blue half cool — body temperature drifts toward whichever side it is on. Green rings are food, fading as they are eaten and regrowing on a cycle. It must feed in the warm half and retreat to cool, so the two drives pull against each other. Orange flashes are innate reflexes firing. The trail is coloured by energy.

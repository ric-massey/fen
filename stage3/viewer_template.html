The warm-to-cool gradient and the food positions are re-drawn every life, so nothing about this world can be memorised — it has to sense which way is warm and read its own temperature to know whether to go there. There is always food on the warm side and food on the cool side, so eating is a choice with a cost rather than a compulsion. Green rings are food, fading as eaten and regrowing on a cycle. Orange flashes are innate reflexes firing. The trail is coloured by energy. The red region is a hazard — it roams, and about 33 steps of contact is fatal. As it closes, the organism's fear rises and the whole view tints: fear is a bodily state, not a signal. While afraid it burns more energy and gets measurably worse at reading its own hunger and temperature — attentional narrowing, so self-knowledge is not constant but varies with how frightened it is.
